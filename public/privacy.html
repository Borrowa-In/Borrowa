Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Email, display name, building/area, item listings and photos, optional approximate location for items, chat messages, borrow history, the IP address you sign in from, and a random device identifier stored in your browser (both used only for abuse prevention).

Why

To run the service, show listings to nearby members, deliver notifications and reminders, rank the leaderboard, and keep the community safe.

Who sees it

Your display name, building and listings are visible to other members, and listings are visible to guests. Other signed-in members can technically see the name, building and email stored on your profile; guests cannot. Your IP address is visible only to admins. Chats are visible to the people in them.

Reported chats

Chats are end-to-end encrypted and unreadable to us. If a member reports a conversation, they choose to share that one chat with admins, who can then read it to review the report. Access is removed when the report is closed.

Services we use

Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Hosting, Cloud Functions, App Check). Data is processed on Google's servers. We do not sell your data.

Retention and your rights

Expired and removed listings are deleted. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your account and data at any time by emailing shouryaupadhyay50@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Borrowa is intended for users 16 and older.
